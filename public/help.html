Getting Started

What is hehe.cx? +
hehe.cx is a link-in-bio platform that lets you create a beautiful, customizable page to share all your social media links in one place. Think of it as your digital business card that you can share anywhere.
How do I create my page? +
Simply sign up with your email, choose a unique username, and you're ready to go! Your page will be available at hehe.cx/yourusername. You can then customize it from your dashboard.
Is hehe.cx free? +
Yes! hehe.cx is free to use with generous limits. Free users can upload images up to 10MB and add unlimited links. Premium users get additional features like 50MB uploads and animated GIF support.

Customization

What can I customize? +
You can customize your display name, bio, profile picture, and background. For backgrounds, you can choose a gradient with custom colors or upload your own image. Premium users can also use animated GIFs.
What platforms can I link? +
We support 19 platforms including Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok, Spotify, Discord, GitHub, LinkedIn, and many more. Each link displays with the platform's official logo.
What is the User ID badge? +
Your User ID shows when you joined hehe.cx. The first person to sign up is #1, the second is #2, and so on. You can toggle this badge on or off in your profile settings.

Premium

What do I get with Premium? +
Premium members get: 50MB upload limit (vs 10MB), animated GIF profile pictures, animated GIF backgrounds, priority support, and early access to new features.
How much does Premium cost? +
Premium is $5/month or $50/year (save $10). Payment integration is coming soon - stay tuned!

Account

Can I change my username? +
Currently, usernames cannot be changed after registration. Please choose carefully when signing up! This feature may be added in the future.
How do I delete my account? +
To delete your account, please contact us at support@example.com. We'll process your request and remove all your data.
